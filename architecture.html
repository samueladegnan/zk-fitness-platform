ZK Fitness

Keep your workout history private. Encrypted on your device, readable only by you.

Launch Live Demo

System Architecture

The system has two tiers:

  • Client: HTML5/Vanilla JS app that handles cryptography, UI, and local state.
  • Server: Node.js/Express API backed by PostgreSQL and containerized with Docker.

Zero-Knowledge Flow

  1. You enter a password on the client.
  2. Argon2id derives an authentication key and an encryption key.
  3. The client encrypts workout data with AES-256-GCM.
  4. The server stores the encrypted blob in PostgreSQL.
  5. On retrieval, the client decrypts the blob locally with your key.
