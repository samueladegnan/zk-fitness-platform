Everything You Need

🔐

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

All workout data is encrypted client-side with AES-256-GCM. The server only sees an opaque blob it cannot decrypt.

🏋️

Strength & Cardio

Log weight and reps for lifting, or distance, duration, heart rate, and calories for running, cycling, swimming, and more.

📊

Gamified Progress

Earn XP, level up, unlock badges, track streaks, and celebrate personal records with confetti and sound effects.

⏱️

Smart Rest Timer

Configurable rest timers with +/-30s quick controls, audio alerts, and warmup set generation.

📝

Plans & History

Create and edit workout templates. View, edit, and delete your full workout history anytime.

🌙

Dark Mode

Toggle between light and dark themes for comfortable tracking in any environment.
