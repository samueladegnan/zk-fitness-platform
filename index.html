Keep your workout history private. Encrypted on your device, readable only by you.

What It Is

ZK Fitness is a privacy-first strength and cardio tracker. It encrypts your data on your device before it reaches the server, so only you can read it.

How It Works

Data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it touches the network. The backend stores only opaque blobs it cannot decrypt. Argon2id derives your encryption key from your password.

Login uses ML-DSA-65 signatures, and each sync wraps a fresh AES key with ML-KEM-768. These NIST post-quantum algorithms protect the platform against future quantum computers.

Key Features

  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture: End-to-end client-side encryption means the server has zero visibility into your personal health data.
  • Post-Quantum Cryptography: ML-DSA-65 authentication and ML-KEM-768 key encapsulation keep data secure against quantum attacks.
  • Workout Logging & Planning: Log strength and cardio sessions, build reusable workout plans, track history, and monitor progress in a clean, user-friendly interface.
  • Cross-Device Sync: Encrypted cloud sync keeps workouts up to date across authenticated devices without exposing data to the server.
  • Offline-First PWA: Works fully offline as a Progressive Web App, with local IndexedDB persistence and mobile install support.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle between light and dark themes for comfortable tracking in any environment.
