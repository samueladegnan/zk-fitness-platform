What It Is

ZK Fitness is a privacy-first, full-stack strength training and cardio tracking platform. It uses client-side encryption and a zero-knowledge cloud architecture to ensure your workout data remains entirely your own.

How It Works

Using the Web Crypto API, all data is encrypted directly in your browser with AES-256-GCM before it touches the network. The backend server only stores opaque blobs it cannot decrypt. Key derivation is handled by Argon2id, providing strong protection against brute-force attacks.

Key Features

  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture: End-to-end client-side encryption means the server has zero visibility into your personal health data.
  • Strength & Cardio Tracking: Log weight and reps for lifting, or distance, duration, heart rate, and calories for running, cycling, swimming, and more.
  • Gamified Progress: Earn XP, level up, unlock badges, and track active streaks.
  • Smart Rest Timer: Configurable rest timers with +/- 30s quick controls, audio alerts, and warmup set generation.
  • Plans & History: Create and edit workout templates, then view, edit, or delete your full workout history.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle between light and dark themes for comfortable tracking in any environment.
